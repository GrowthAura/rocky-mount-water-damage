Why Rocky Mount Homes Need a Local Water Damage Team

Rocky Mount knows water. When Hurricane Floyd pushed the Tar River to a record crest in 1999, roughly a quarter of the city was under water and thousands of homes were damaged. Hurricane Matthew brought the river up again in 2016. Between the big storms, the everyday problems are just as real: crawl spaces that stay damp all summer, AC units that overflow into ceilings, septic and sewer backups, and manufactured homes that need a different kind of care.

We built our service around those realities. That means always checking the crawl space, drying with the humidity in mind, handling floodwater safely, and working with homeowners, landlords and businesses across both Nash and Edgecombe County. crawl space water damage

How Our Rocky Mount Crew Protects Your Home While We Work

A water damage job means equipment, hoses and people moving through your home for days. We take simple steps to protect the rest of your house.

  • Floor protection on the paths between the door and the work area.
  • Plastic barriers to keep dust and moisture out of dry rooms.
  • Furniture moved, blocked up or covered instead of left on wet floors.
  • Crawl space access kept clean and closed when we are not working.
  • Daily cleanup before we leave.

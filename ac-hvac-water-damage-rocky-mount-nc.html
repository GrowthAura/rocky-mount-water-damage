How AC Systems Leak in Rocky Mount Homes

In Rocky Mount, air conditioners often run from late spring well into fall, and on a muggy day a single system can pull gallons of water out of the air. All of that water has to go somewhere. When something in the path fails, it ends up in your home instead.

Here is where we see AC water damage most often in the Twin Counties:

  • Attic air handlers. Many homes have the indoor unit in the attic. A clogged drain line or rusted drain pan sends water straight into the ceiling insulation and drywall below.
  • Closet units. Indoor units in hall closets can leak onto hardwood, carpet and the subfloor, and the water may drip into the crawl space.
  • Sweating ducts in crawl spaces. Cold supply ducts in a hot, humid crawl space collect condensation, especially if insulation is torn or missing. The drips soak insulation and the vapor barrier.
  • Frozen coils. A dirty filter or low refrigerant can freeze the coil. When it thaws, more water than the pan can handle pours out at once.
  • Disconnected or crushed ducts. Ducts knocked loose under the house blow cold air into the crawl space, where it hits humid air and creates wet, moldy wood.

Because these leaks are slow and out of sight, they often go on for weeks. By the time a stain appears, insulation and framing are soaked and mold may already be growing.

Why Does an AC System Cause Water Damage in Rocky Mount Homes?

Short answer: a central air conditioner pulls moisture out of the air and sends it down a condensate drain line. When that line clogs with algae or debris, the drain pan overflows, and when ducts run through humid crawl spaces, they sweat. In Rocky Mount's long, humid cooling season, either problem can soak ceilings, attic insulation or crawl space framing within days. Shut the system off if water is pouring and call for drying.

Warning Signs of an AC Leak in Your Rocky Mount Home

  • A yellow or brown stain spreading on the ceiling below the attic air handler
  • Water dripping from a ceiling vent or light fixture when the AC is running
  • A wet floor or carpet around an indoor unit in a hall closet
  • A musty smell when the system kicks on
  • Water pouring from the secondary drain line over a window or eave, which means the main line is clogged
  • Soaked or sagging insulation under ducts in the crawl space
  • Higher indoor humidity and a sticky feel even with the AC running

How to Prevent AC Leaks in Rocky Mount Homes

  • Have your system serviced every spring, before the long cooling season starts.
  • Ask your technician to flush the condensate line and check the drain pan for rust or cracks.
  • Add a float switch on the drain pan that shuts the system off if water backs up. Many attic units should have one.
  • Pour a cup of distilled vinegar down the condensate line access every few months to slow algae growth, if your HVAC company recommends it.
  • Replace air filters on schedule; a clogged filter can freeze the coil, and the melt overflows the pan.
  • Make sure ductwork in the crawl space is fully insulated and sealed, with no torn wrap.
  • Keep crawl space humidity under control with a sealed vapor barrier or dehumidifier so ducts do not sweat.
  • Put a water alarm in the drain pan or under indoor units.

Attic Air Handlers: Why Rocky Mount Ceilings Leak in July

Many Rocky Mount homes have the indoor AC unit, called the air handler, in the attic. In July and August, these units remove gallons of water from the air every day, and that water has to drain somewhere.

  • The primary drain line carries condensate outside or to a drain; algae can clog it.
  • The secondary drain pan under the unit catches overflow, but it can rust or crack.
  • A float switch shuts the unit off when the pan fills, protecting the ceiling below.
  • Attic heat can warp plastic pans and loosen fittings over time.
  • A drip from a pipe over a window or door means the backup drain is working and the main line is clogged.

If a ceiling stain appears under your air handler, turn the system off and call Water Damage Rocky Mount before the drywall gives way.

Mini-Splits, Window Units and Dehumidifier Leaks in Rocky Mount

Central systems are not the only cooling equipment that leaks in Rocky Mount homes. Smaller units cause their own water damage.

  • Ductless mini-splits: indoor heads drip down walls when their drain line clogs or the line is not sloped right.
  • Window units: tilted the wrong way, they drain inside onto sills and walls.
  • Portable dehumidifiers: full buckets or failed shutoff switches overflow onto floors.
  • Condensate pumps: small pumps fail and overflow without warning.
  • Walk-in coolers and ice machines in businesses can leak under floors.

AC & HVAC Water Damage FAQs for Rocky Mount, NC

The AC water damage questions Rocky Mount homeowners ask most.

Why are the ducts under my house dripping water?

When cold supply ducts run through a hot, humid crawl space, moisture in the air condenses on the outside of the duct, the same way a cold glass sweats on a summer day. Torn or thin duct insulation makes it worse. The drips soak the vapor barrier and insulation below, and over a season they can cause as much damage as a plumbing leak.

Should I keep running my AC if it is leaking?

If the leak is only at the unit and the ducts are dry, you can usually keep running it while a technician clears the drain. If water is pouring through a ceiling, ducts are wet or sitting in water, or the unit is in standing water, turn it off at the thermostat and the breaker until it is checked.

Does insurance cover AC leak water damage?

Sudden, accidental water damage, such as an overflowing drain pan that soaked a ceiling, is often covered. Slow, ongoing condensation and lack of maintenance are commonly excluded. Ask your agent, and we will document what failed and what got wet.

Do you repair the AC unit itself?

Our crew handles the water damage: drying, cleaning, mold control and repairs to ceilings, walls, insulation and floors. The AC unit, drain line and ductwork repairs are done by your HVAC contractor, and we are happy to coordinate with them so the leak and the damage are fixed together.
